# Acceptable Use Policy — draft 2026-09-20
Use the service only for authorized company research and lawful pilot evaluation.
Do not bypass quotas, share credentials, upload malicious files, impersonate entities,
falsify correction evidence, probe other accounts without permission, or redistribute
restricted source data. Do not use the research score as an automated consumer
credit, housing, employment, insurance or other eligibility decision.
CSV uploads must contain company identifiers required for research; do not include
payment credentials or unnecessary personal data. Respect source access limits and
vendor consent requirements. Report suspected security issues through the configured
security contact. Operator enforcement, appeals and notification procedures require
final owner/counsel approval. No right to use a third-party service is created here.
